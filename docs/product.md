# Product foundation

## Objective

Enable an artist-director to produce complete animated or photorealistic short
stories and client advertisements in a single project workspace. The founding
artist works primarily in pencil. His drawings must be usable as creative source
material, not merely as disposable prompts.

The long-term business is a multi-tenant production platform for independent
creators, studios, and brand teams. Subscription, metered generation, and managed
production are business hypotheses, not validated pricing or revenue claims.

## Shared creative workflow

1. Create a project and choose animation or photorealistic mode.
2. Upload original material and record ownership or permission.
3. Develop a brief, story, script, characters, and world references.
4. Split scenes into ordered shots with duration and camera directions.
5. Approve storyboard revisions and generation budgets.
6. Generate candidate takes and select or replace individual shots.
7. Assemble selected takes, dialogue, music, and captions.
8. Review a version, resolve comments, and export the approved cut.

## Mode-specific controls

Animation needs character sheets, pencil texture references, composition control,
and explicit style constraints. Photorealistic filmmaking needs fictional casting,
wardrobe, location, lighting, and continuity references. Consistent identity and
style are quality goals requiring evaluation, not guaranteed model capabilities.

## First working release

A director can create one 15–30 second story containing at least three shots and
one recurring character, revise one shot without replacing the others, and export
a playable MP4. Both production modes use the same project and revision model.
The visual quality of each mode must be assessed separately.

Required workspace capabilities:

- Project list, creative brief, asset upload, and character reference library.
- Editable script, ordered scenes and shots, storyboard images, and take selection.
- Asynchronous generation with visible state, estimate, limit, and actual usage.
- Basic cut assembly, uploaded or generated audio, captions, and versioned export.
- Owner, editor, and reviewer permissions with authenticated client review.
- English, Latvian, and Russian interface text and errors.

## Outside the first release

Feature-length autonomous filmmaking, custom model training, a public creator
marketplace, advanced compositing, real-time collaborative editing, and dedicated
mobile applications. These are not prerequisites for the first complete workflow.

## Rights and privacy

Keep original art and client assets private by default. Record rights assertions
and permission for recognizable people and voices. Do not offer impersonation as
a default workflow. Establish deletion, retention, provider data-use disclosures,
and export rights before onboarding external customers. Do not assume that an
input license or model subscription grants every commercial output right.
